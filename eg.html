Strategic Air Command Elite Guard

The Strategic Air Command Elite Guard is the most elite guarding unit the Air Force has. The job of SACEG is to provide security to the Air Force General Command+, Directorate of the Security Forces, and personal protection for the Commander and Vice Commander of the unit. SACEG is the only unit that can guard Air Force Command, Air Force High Command, and AFSF HC.

Role & Responsibilities

The Elite Guards are the main guarding unit of AFSF, attending tryouts and regular AF events on a daily basis ensuring that it's protected from raiders and any interruptions. They also maintain strict ceremonial standards, execute formal drills, and serve as the public-facing symbol of AFSF professionalism during official events and divisional inspections.
