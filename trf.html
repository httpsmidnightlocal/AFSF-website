Tactical Response Force

TRF or Tactical Response Force is the most elite combat and guarding unit inside of AF. They are in charge of doing missions deemed too hard for normal AFSF personnel to handle and they are in charge guarding the high executives of Air Force like General Command and STRATCOM.
